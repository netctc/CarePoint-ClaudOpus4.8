# Sustained Operations Vendor and Knowledge Readiness V37

V37 covers two operating risks that appear after technical-debt and post-incident governance are in place:

1. Critical vendor resilience: recent provider status, SLA evidence, incident state and tested exit/contingency plans.
2. Knowledge transfer readiness: reviewed runbooks, on-call ownership, rollback/privacy topics, training completion and handoff checklists.

Both reviews intentionally operate on metadata-only evidence. They should be used as release/traffic-expansion gates before declaring stable sustained operation.
