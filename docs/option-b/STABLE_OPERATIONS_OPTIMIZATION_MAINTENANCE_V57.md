# Stable Operations Optimization and Maintenance - V57

V57 transitions the project from post-closure stabilization into a governed recurring operating rhythm. It keeps Python in an advisory role while operators retain ownership of all execution.

## Stable operations optimization

The optimization review checks whether the transferred steady-state operation has enough safe evidence to consider controlled improvements. It evaluates optimization metrics, cost signals, reliability signals, automation opportunities, debt items, guardrails, approvals and evidence.

A `pass` means the optimization backlog can move into normal operator-owned governance. A `hold` or `rollback` blocks execution until the missing or failing evidence is corrected.

## Recurring maintenance cycle readiness

The maintenance readiness review checks whether the team can safely enter a recurring maintenance cadence. It validates maintenance windows, patch cadence, dependency update planning, backup validation, runbook schedule, owner roster, approvals and evidence.

A `pass` means the recurring maintenance cadence can begin under operator control. A `hold` or `rollback` means maintenance governance needs more evidence or remediation before adoption.

## Guardrails

- No calendar mutation.
- No patch execution.
- No dependency upgrade execution.
- No backup or restore execution.
- No secret rotation.
- No owner reassignment.
- No ticket, budget, infrastructure, SLO, alert or risk-record mutation.
