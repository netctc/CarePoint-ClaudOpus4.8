# Final Handover and Phase Closure V64

V64 formalizes the last step of the Option B Python progressive implementation phase.

## Final operational handover review

The final handover review verifies, in metadata-only advisory form, that:

- final and rollback runbooks are ready;
- SRE/support/product owners are assigned;
- support model is approved;
- monitoring controls are healthy;
- escalation paths are validated;
- operational risks are accepted or closed;
- required signoffs and evidence are present.

## Phase closure certification review

The phase closure certification review verifies, in metadata-only advisory form, that:

- closure criteria are met;
- evidence package is complete;
- handover evidence is approved;
- residual risks are accepted or closed;
- release artifacts are present and verified;
- approvals are complete;
- future work is separated into a post-closure backlog.

## Boundary

Python remains advisory and dry-run only. It does not certify the release, mutate tickets, assign owners, accept risks, activate support rotations, publish handover material, tag production releases or close the phase in any system of record.

## Recommendation

After V64, do not keep extending this phase. Start a new phase only for real corrective defects or for planned post-closure enhancements.
