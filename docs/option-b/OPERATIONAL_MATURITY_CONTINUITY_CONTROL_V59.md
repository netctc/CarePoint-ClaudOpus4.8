# Operational Maturity and Continuity Control V59

V59 extends the post-maintenance steady-state track with two governance reviews that support long-running operations.

## Recurring operational maturity audit

Checks sanitized evidence for maturity dimensions, controls, incident learnings, support signals, operator evidence, risks and approvals. It provides a `pass`, `hold` or `rollback` recommendation for recurring governance.

## Stable-state continuity control

Checks sanitized continuity controls, DR signals, dependency continuity, operational fallbacks, communication checks, risks and approvals. It provides a `pass`, `hold` or `rollback` recommendation for continuity governance.

## Non-mutation guarantee

Python remains advisory only. Operators and Node-owned workflows continue to own all durable state, remediation execution, risk acceptance, ticketing, communications and business-continuity operations.
