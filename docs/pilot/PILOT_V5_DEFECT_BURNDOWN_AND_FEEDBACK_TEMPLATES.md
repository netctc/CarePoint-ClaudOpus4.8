# PILOT-V5 - Defect Burn-down and Feedback Templates

## Defect register fields

- defect id;
- title;
- severity;
- workflow;
- role affected;
- environment;
- owner;
- status;
- reproduction steps;
- expected result;
- actual result;
- evidence link;
- target resolution date;
- waiver or workaround;
- expansion impact.

## Feedback record fields

- feedback id;
- source role;
- workflow;
- sentiment;
- friction point;
- blocker yes/no;
- support interaction yes/no;
- recommendation;
- disposition;
- owner;
- decision impact.

## Adoption evidence record fields

- cohort;
- active users;
- workflows completed;
- drop-offs;
- support touches;
- role acceptance note;
- audit/data confidence note;
- final recommendation.
